Create an eight-second vertical 9:16 cinematic social advertising film for AURIA, a fictional premium Brazilian architecture and interiors studio. Use the attached image as the visual reference for the interior design, materials and palette: ivory upholstery, warm travertine, walnut furniture and tropical greenery. The film must demonstrate a home being lived in, with clear physical action and three distinct shots connected by clean hard cuts.

CAST: The adult woman in the attached image, with shoulder-length dark hair, wearing an understated cream linen shirt and taupe trousers. Keep her appearance and clothing consistent across the shots. Continue her curtain-opening gesture immediately from the reference image; do not hold on a frozen first frame. Natural movement, no posing at the camera, no dialogue. Avoid facial close-ups. Adapt the framing to the action rather than freezing the reference composition.

SHOT 1 — 0.0 to 2.5 seconds: Medium-wide view near the floor-to-ceiling window in this living room. Start with the woman already moving, pulling the sheer curtain aside with one hand. The action must be obvious in the first second. Keep exposure natural and stable; no magical change of sunlight direction.

HARD CUT.

SHOT 2 — 2.5 to 5.0 seconds: Tactile close-up at the walnut coffee table. The same woman places a ceramic cup on a small stone coaster beside a closed book, then withdraws her hand. HARD CUT.

SHOT 3 — 5.0 to 8.0 seconds: Wider view of the same living room. The same woman lowers herself naturally onto the ivory sofa, opens a book already in her hands and settles into the space. STYLE: Premium architectural lifestyle commercial, believable anatomy, coherent spatial design, natural afternoon light, realistic motion and restrained warm colors. Energetic through purposeful action, changing shot scales and editing, without frantic effects. The final film must make sense when muted. DO NOT DELIVER: One continuous slow zoom, an empty room with only camera movement, a slideshow of still images, freeze-frame opening, slow-motion footage throughout, speed ramps, spinning camera, whip transitions, dissolves, morphing furniture, changing architecture, exaggerated lens effects, flashing light, generated text, subtitles, logos or watermarks.
